\begin{abstract}
We forecast daily Revenue and COGS for 2023--2024 (548 days) using an ensemble of Theta decomposition (17.4\%) and Hybrid ARIMA (82.6\%), validated via expanding-window cross-validation on 2020--2022. Cross-validated performance: MAE = 522,663 VND, RMSE = 731,302 VND, $R^2 = 0.7778$. Forecast total: 2.322B VND Revenue and 2.109B VND COGS, with ±12\% typical daily error. No external data, no test leakage, full reproducibility.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{Cyclic Encoding}

\[
\text{doy\_sin} = \sin\left(\frac{2\pi \cdot \text{doy}}{365.25}\right), \quad
\text{doy\_cos} = \cos\left(\frac{2\pi \cdot \text{doy}}{365.25}\right)
\]

Rationale: Raw integer features (doy $\in [1,365]$) create false discontinuity at year boundary. Cyclic sin/cos encoding preserves ordinal distance.

\section{Cross-Validation Methodology}

Expanding-window CV (3 folds):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Fold 2020}: Train 2012-07-04 to 2019-12-31, validate 2020
  \item \textbf{Fold 2021}: Train 2012-07-04 to 2020-12-31, validate 2021
  \item \textbf{Fold 2022}: Train 2012-07-04 to 2021-12-31, validate 2022
\end{itemize}

Why expanding, not rolling? Rolling window discards history and loses long-run growth trend. Expanding preserves all history—critical for reliable extrapolation to 2023--2024.

\section{Mathematical Formulas}

\subsection{Competition Metrics}

\[
\text{MAE} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} |F_i - A_i|, \quad
\text{RMSE} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (F_i - A_i)^2}, \quad
R^2 = 1 - \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(A_i - F_i)^2}{\sum_{i=1}^{n}(A_i - \bar{A})^2}
\]

where $n = 1,096$ (Revenue and COGS concatenated).

\subsection{Theta Method}

Decomposes $y_t$ into two theta lines: $\tilde{y}_0(t) = 2\bar{y} - y_t$ and $\tilde{y}_2(t) = y_t$.

Forecast: $F(h) = \tfrac{1}{2}\,\text{SES}(\tilde{y}_0, h) + \tfrac{1}{2}(a + b(T+h))$

Applied on $\log(1+y)$ with learned day-of-week multiplier.

\subsection{Hybrid ARIMA}

\textbf{Step 1 --- Seasonal Baseline:} $\hat{y}(t) = b \cdot g^{\Delta_{\text{yr}}} \cdot s(\text{month}, \text{day})$

\textbf{Step 2 --- Residual ARIMA$(p,0,q)$:} $e_t = c + \sum_{i=1}^{p} \varphi_i e_{t-i} + \sum_{j=1}^{q} \theta_j \varepsilon_{t-j} + \varepsilon_t$

\textbf{Step 3 --- Combine:} $F(h) = \max(0, \hat{y}(h) + \text{ARIMA\_forecast}(h))$
